# Designer Maturity Intelligence

**Evidence-based professional maturity assessment and personal development operating system.**

Designer Maturity Intelligence (DMI) helps designers assess professional capabilities using evidence, separate strategic thinking from execution and impact, identify career positioning, and turn findings into a sustainable 30/60/90-day development plan.

> **Important:** DMI is a structured reflection framework, not a psychometric test, formal HR certification, or independently validated industry benchmark. Scores are estimates and must reflect the evidence available.

## Core principles

- **Evidence before scoring:** distinguish direct, self-reported, behavioral, inferred, and missing evidence.
- **Thinking / Execution / Impact:** evaluate each competency in three separate dimensions.
- **No invented proof:** ambition, implementation, adoption, and measurable impact are different evidence levels.
- **Transparent uncertainty:** include confidence and limitations; missing evidence is not automatically a capability gap.
- **Consistent scoring:** use the defined 0–10 scale and domain weighting where evidence supports a score.
- **Development through application:** connect each priority to real work, feedback, artifacts, and review milestones.
- **Sustainable scope:** no more than three active development priorities.
- **Human confirmation for visuals:** finish the written report before asking whether to generate the JPG infographic.
- **Longitudinal continuity:** compare future evidence with the original baseline; never fabricate historical scores.

11. Limitations and Confidence
12. Final Written Conclusion
13. Final Numerical Dataset
14. Personal Development Coaching Summary
15. Personal Development Operating System

## Eight competency domains

- Design Strategy & Systems Thinking
- User Research & Experience Quality
- Product Thinking & Business Impact
- Interaction & Visual Design
- Design Systems & DesignOps
- Technical & Delivery Capability
- AI, Automation & Innovation
- Leadership, Communication & Mentorship

Each domain is assessed through Thinking, Execution, and Impact. The domain formula is:

**Domain Score = (Thinking × 0.40) + (Execution × 0.40) + (Impact × 0.20)**

Score bands: 0–1.9 Not demonstrated; 2.0–3.9 Emerging; 4.0–5.4 Developing; 5.5–6.9 Proficient; 7.0–8.4 Advanced; 8.5–9.4 Expert; 9.5–10 Exceptional. These are framework guidelines, not industry norms.

## Maturity levels

1. Foundational
2. Developing
3. Independent
4. Strategic / Systemic
5. Transformational

Level 5 requires evidence of sustained adoption, repeatability, organizational influence, and impact—not just sophisticated proposals or tools.

## Personal development cycle

**Assess → Prioritize → Plan → Practice → Apply → Receive feedback → Reflect → Reassess → Adapt**

Track learning progress, behavioral progress, and outcome progress separately. Completing a course or attempting an activity is not sufficient evidence of improved professional performance.
